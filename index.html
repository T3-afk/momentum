Permanent Tasks
Permanent tasks are high-stakes goals. Completing one awards Rank Points only. Failing one deducts your chosen Rank Points and also applies your chosen daily penalty to the selected category on the failure day. Deadline tasks fail automatically after the deadline; tasks without a deadline can only fail when you mark them failed.
